LAW ON THE APPLICATION OF CIVIL AND COMMERCIAL LAWS IN THE DIFC
PART 1: GENERAL
Title
1.
This Law may be cited as the "Law on the Application of Civil and Commercial Laws in the DIFC 2004".
2.
Legislative Authority
This Law is made by the Ruler of Dubai.
Application of the Law
3.
This Law applies in the jurisdiction of the Dubai International Financial Centre.
Date of enactment
4.
This Law is enacted on the date specified in the Enactment Notice in respect of this Law.
5.
Commencement
This Law comes into force on the date specified in the Enactment Notice in respect of this Law.
6.
Interpretation
Schedule 1 contains a list of defined terms used in this Law.
7.
The objectives of this Law
The objectives of this Law are to:
provide certainty as to the rights, liabilities and obligations of persons in relation to civil and
(a)
commercial matters arising in the DIFC;
assist in the development of the non-statutory law of the DIFC through decisions of the DIFC
(b)
Courts; and
allow persons to adopt the laws of another jurisdiction in relation to civil and commercial matters
(c)
arising within the DIFC.

PART 2: APPLICATION OF LAWS
8.
Choice of applicable law
Since by virtue of Article 3 of Federal Law No. 8 of 2004, DIFC Law is able to apply in the DIFC
(1)
notwithstanding any Federal Law on civil or commercial matters, the rights and liabilities between
persons in any civil or commercial matter are to be determined according to the laws for the time
being in force in the Jurisdiction chosen in accordance with paragraph (2).
The relevant Jurisdiction is to be the one first ascertained under the following paragraphs:
(2)
so far as there is a regulatory content, any applicable DIFC Statute; failing which,
(a)
the law of any Jurisdiction other than that of the DIFC expressly chosen by any DIFC
(b)
Statute; failing which,
the laws of a Jurisdiction as agreed between all the relevant persons concerned in the
(c)
matter; failing which,
the laws of any Jurisdiction which appears to the DIFC Court or Arbitrator to be the one
(d)
most closely related to the facts of and the persons concerned in the matter; failing which,
DIFC Law.
(e)
8A.
Content of DIFC Law
The following provisions apply where DIFC Law is the law applicable to a civil or commercial
(1)
matter pursuant to Article 8 above.
The content of DIFC Law shall be determined by any applicable DIFC Statute, and any DIFC Court
(2)
judgments interpreting and applying the applicable DIFC Statute in a manner consistent with this
Law.
The common law (including the principles and rules of equity) supplements DIFC Statute except
(3)
to the extent modified by this Law or any other DIFC Law. The DIFC Courts in determining the
common law for the DIFC in any case may have regard to the common law of England and Wales
and other common law jurisdictions.
The common law of the DIFC (including the principles and rules of equity), as determined by the
(4)
DIFC Courts, must not be inconsistent with DIFC Statute.
8B.
Interpretation of DIFC Statutes
The interpretation of DIFC Statutes may be guided by:
(1)
jurisprudence from common law jurisdictions regarding the interpretation and application
(a)
of analogous laws; and
(b)
the rules and principles of statutory interpretation from common law jurisdictions.
Article 8B(1) applies to all DIFC Statutes, regardless of whether the relevant DIFC Statute is based
(2)
on an international model law or another non-common law source.
If a DIFC Statute is based on an international model law, its interpretation may also be guided by
(3)
international jurisprudence interpreting and applying the international model law, as well as
interpretative aids and commentary published by international bodies regarding the international
model law.

SCHEDULE 1
INTERPRETATION
1.
Rules of interpretation
In the Law, a reference to:
(1)
a statutory provision includes a reference to the statutory provision as amended or re-enacted from
(a)
time to time;
(b)
a person includes any natural person, body corporate or body unincorporate, including a company,
partnership, unincorporated association, government or state;
an obligation to publish or cause to be published a particular document shall, unless expressly
(c)
provided otherwise in the Law, include publishing or causing to be published in printed or
electronic form;
a day shall refer to a business day, being a calendar day, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and official
(d)
public holidays;
a calendar year shall mean a year of the Gregorian calendar;
(e)
a reference to the masculine gender includes the feminine.
(f)
The headings in the Law shall not affect its interpretation.
(2)
References in this Law to a body corporate include a body corporate incorporated outside DIFC.
(3)
A reference in this Law to a Part, Article or Schedule by number only, and without further identification, is
(4)
a reference to the Part, Article or Schedule of that number in this Law.
A reference in an Article or other division of this Law to a paragraph, sub-paragraph or Article by number
(5)
or letter only, and without further identification, is a reference to the paragraph, sub-paragraph or Article of
that number or letter contained in the Article or other division of this Law in which that reference occurs.
Unless the context otherwise requires, where this Law refers to an enactment, the reference is to that
(6)
enactment as amended from time to time, and includes a reference to that enactment as extended or applied
by or under another enactment, including any other provision of that enactment.
References to this Law to a writing, filing, instrument or certificate include any mode of communication that
(7)
preserves a record of the information contained therein and is capable of being reproduced in tangible form,
including electronic means.
