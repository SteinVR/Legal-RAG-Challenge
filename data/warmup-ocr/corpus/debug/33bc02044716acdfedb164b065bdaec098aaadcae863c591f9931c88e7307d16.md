1.
Title and repeal
This Employment Law 2019, repeals and replaces the Employment Law 2005 (DIFC Law No. 4 of
(1)
2005), as it was in force immediately prior to the commencement of this Law (the "Previous Law"),
and may be cited as the "Employment Law 2019" or "this Law".
Except where otherwise provided in this Law, anything done or omitted to be done pursuant to or
(2)
for the purposes of the Previous Law is deemed to be done or omitted to be done pursuant to or for
the purposes of this Law.
Without limiting the generality of Article 1(2), and subject only to Articles 1(4), 10 and 61(2), the
(3)
repeal and replacement under Article 1(1) shall not affect:
any right, remedy, debt or obligation accrued to or incurred by any person; or
(a)
any legal proceeding commenced, or to be commenced, in respect of any such right,
(b)
remedy, debt or obligation,
under the Previous Law, and any such legal proceeding must be instituted, continued or enforced,
including any penalty, fine or forfeiture, under this Law without prejudice to any right, remedy,
debt or obligation which has accrued or incurred prior to the commencement of this Law.
Where there is no equivalent provision in this Law to a provision in the Previous Law, the relevant
(4)
provision in the Previous Law is deemed to survive the repeal and replacement under Article 1(1),
until such time as necessary for the purposes of any legal proceeding specified in Article 1(3)(b).
The fact that a provision in this Law reduces or extinguishes rights in the Previous Law does not
prevent it from being an equivalent provision.

8.
This Law and any Regulations made under it shall be administered by the DIFCA.

The power of the DIFCA to make Regulations and impose fines and penalties
The Board of Directors of the DIFCA may:
(1)

(a)

impose fines or penalties or both for any contraventions of this Law; and

(2)

Limitation Period
Subject to Article 20(2) and Article 61(2), a Court shall not consider a claim under this Law unless it is
presented to the Court either during an Employee's employment with an Employer or not later than six (6)
months after the relevant Employee's Termination Date.
